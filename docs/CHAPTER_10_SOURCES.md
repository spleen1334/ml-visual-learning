## Source boundaries and cautions

- `../Learning Files/3. Maven_ML_Demos_COMPLETE.xlsx` contains no Chapter 10 / model-selection worksheet. Its available tabs cover QA, profiling, individual classification demos (KNN, naïve Bayes, decision trees, logistic regression and sentiment), and later topics. Do not present Chapter 10 figures as workbook outputs.
- `10.6 Model Selection & Tuning.html` is a scored quiz page (`quiz_title: Model Selection & Tuning`), not a lesson or data resource. Project instructions exclude quizzes from the learning companion.
- The PDF's p183 “weighted avg” accuracy of `.9822` is a support-weighted average of one-vs-rest accuracies, which is not the usual overall multi-class accuracy. From its displayed matrix, overall accuracy is diagonal sum / total = (214+452+1123+34)/1886 = 1823/1886 ≈ **.9666**. The printed weighted recall `.9666` does reconcile with the matrix: support-weighted recall equals overall accuracy for this single-label matrix. Preserve the source figures only with an explicit note or recompute correctly; do not label `.9822` overall accuracy.
- The course presents balancing the training data as a way to address class imbalance. If demonstrating it, keep resampling within training folds/splits; never duplicate or remove observations using the held-out test set. Evaluate on data representative of the real class prevalence, and explain that up/down-sampling changes training composition rather than changing the underlying event rate.
- Treat the nuclear-reactor, spam/search and product examples as instructional scenarios. The PDF’s multi-class counts are supplied examples, not a documented real-world dataset. The source’s “best model” is context-dependent, not a universal ranking.
