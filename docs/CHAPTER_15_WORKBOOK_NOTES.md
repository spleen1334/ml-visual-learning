## Workbook calculation details

- **ACF:** named range `Actual` is `Auto Correlation!C4:C123`; `Lag_1` through `Lag_36` map to D:AM rows 4:123. The correlation formula begins in `AQ4` as `CORREL(Actual,INDIRECT(AO4))` and fills through `AQ39`. The worksheet labels the 36-lag plot's x values `AN4:AN39` and correlation values `AQ4:AQ39`. The series shows annual recurrence at lag 12, 24 and 36.
- **One-Hot:** the omitted January category is the reference month; D:N encode February through December. The ToolPak output has 58 training observations (2015 Jan–2019 Oct). Forecast cells use `P4 = $S$37 + SUMPRODUCT(D4:N4,TRANSPOSE($S$38:$S$48))`, filled through row 75. `R37:R48` labels the intercept and month terms; S:Z contain coefficient, standard error, t statistic, p value and confidence limits. `P4:P75` is the fitted/forecast result; the chart-source prediction is in `AB:AC` and `AC` is controlled by `AE3`.
- **Seasonality + Trend:** observed monthly gym revenue (`P`) runs January 2010–October 2019; rows 122–135 forecast November 2019–December 2020. The no-trend fit has 118 observations, 11 month dummies (Jan–Nov; December is reference), and intercept at `U45`; forecast formula in `Q4` is `$U$45+SUMPRODUCT(D4:N4*TRANSPOSE($U$46:$U$56))`. Trend model adds time-step `O` with coefficient `U90`; its formula in `R4` is `$U$78+SUMPRODUCT(D4:O4*TRANSPOSE($U$79:$U$90))`. The trend coefficient is `0.0212713031649598` ($MM per month); fit R² is `0.9665214802`, versus `0.0834711885` with no trend. Main chart source ranges stop at October 2020 (`AP/AQ133`), while formulas/cached forecasts continue through December 2020 (`Q/R135`). Preserve the chart's source extent if reproducing it exactly; the last two forecast months exist in the worksheet but are not in that chart series.
- **Smoothing:** six formulas are centered moving averages over the observed daily rating column: 7, 14, 21, 28, 42 or 84 values. For example, `D7=AVERAGE($C4:$C10)` and `E10=AVERAGE($C4:$C17)`. Named ranges `_1week`, `_2week`, `_3week`, `_4week`, `_6week`, `_12week` point to the corresponding calculated columns. `S3` selects the visible window; `S2` computes the named range, and the chart uses `moving_avg=INDIRECT(Smoothing!$S$2)`. All 1,095 raw ratings and all cached moving-average values are retained.
- **Non-Linear Trend:** model equation is `A*EXP(-B*week*EXP(-C*week))`, cached in D4:D27; error is `(model−actual)^2` in E4:E11. Only the first eight weeks have actual sales (3, 11, 32, 110, 174, 412, 452, 793 units); rows 12–27 are future model values, not observed sales. Source SSE is 850,490.7208382974. Parameters A/B/C default to 900/7/0.4 and are interactive worksheet values, not a claim that the displayed model is the fitted optimum. The source chart's actual series ends on row 13 while the model extends through row 27.
- **Intervention:** dates run 2020-01-01–2020-03-31. The intervention is the `Original` landing page through 2020-03-06 (66 observations), followed by `New` from 2020-03-07 (25). The regression is trained on rows 4:69 only: intercept plus Sun–Fri dummies (Saturday omitted) and time step; output coefficients are `R80:R87`, with fit summary at `Q66:R71`. Forecast is `M4=$R$80+SUMPRODUCT(F4:L4,TRANSPOSE($R$81:$R$87))` and fills through M94. Incremental conversions per day are `N70=(D70*E70)-(D70*M70)` and cumulative total is O70 onward. The workbook charts call these "incremental daily sales" and "cumulative impact"; the calculation uses sessions × conversion-rate differences, so the values are conversion counts.
