## Adaptation decisions

- Keep this page brief and orienting rather than teaching regression mechanics or a fitted model.
- The app’s definition distinguishes regression as a numeric estimate from forecasting as a future estimate using time-ordered observations. This supplies a clear learner-facing distinction while staying within the announced Part 3 scope.
- The home-price and monthly-sales panels are synthetic teaching illustrations. They are not course-workbook outputs and do not claim a trained or validated model.
- The roadmap previews the next course units: Intro to Regression, Regression Modeling 101, Model Diagnostics, and Time-Series Forecasting. It is not a quiz, gate, or claim that their content is already implemented.
